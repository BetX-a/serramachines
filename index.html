About Serra

Established in 2004, Serra Machines is a trusted name in industrial AC induction motors and centrifugal blowers. Located in the heart of GIDC Makarpura, Vadodara, under the brand "BENZ", we engineer and manufacture high-performance machinery built to meet the most demanding industrial standards.

From standard off-the-shelf motors to fully bespoke blower systems, our team brings decades of hands-on expertise to every project we undertake.
